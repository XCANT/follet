# Code of Conduct

Follet is a small project made for fun. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language (English or French are both fine).
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent.
- Accept that the maintainer may say no to a feature, and explain why when they do.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, slurs, or sexual or violent content.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam and self-promotion unrelated to Follet.

## Where it applies

In the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through **Security → Report a vulnerability** on the GitHub repository, and start the title with "Conduct". Only the maintainer sees these reports, and they stay confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block people who break these rules, starting with a warning except in serious cases.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
